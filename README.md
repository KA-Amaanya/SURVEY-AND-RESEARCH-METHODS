# Statistics: Sample Surveys and Research Design

Welcome to this repository! This project contains coursework, notes, or implementations focused on the fundamental methodologies of sample surveys and structured research design.

## 📋 Course Overview

This repository covers the foundational and advanced concepts required to design, execute, and analyze statistical surveys and research experiments. 

### Key Topics Covered:
* **Sample Surveys & Survey Methods:** Understanding the principles of selecting representative subsets from a population and implementing standard survey methodologies.
* **Sampling Errors:** Identifying, quantifying, and minimizing both sampling and non-sampling errors to ensure data accuracy.
* **Sample Size Estimation:** Applying statistical formulas to determine the optimal sample size required for valid confidence intervals and statistical power.
* **Research Design:** Structuring experimental and observational research frameworks to ensure internal and external validity.
